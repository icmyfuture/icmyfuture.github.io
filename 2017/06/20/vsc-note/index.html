快捷键

editor.action.insertCursorAtEndOfEachLineSelected: 选中多行，添加光标至行末 shift+alt/command+L
selectAllSearchEditorMatches: 选中文本，光标选中所有匹配文本 shift+alt/command+U
claudeSessionsSidebarView.focus: 显示claude session view shift+alt/command+6
showActiveFileInExplorer: 在explorer中定位当前文件 shift+alt/command+7
focusActiveEditorGroup: 切换到当前编辑器窗口 shift+alt/command+8
workbench.action.chat.open: 打开chat窗口 shit+alt/command+9
agentSessions.toggleAgentSessionsSidebar: 打开/关闭chat session侧边栏 shift+alt+ctrl/command+9
workbench.action.chat.openInEditor: 将chat窗口移动到编辑器 shift+alt+ctrl/command+<
workbench.action.chat.openInSidebar: 将chat窗口移动到侧边栏 shift+alt+ctrl/command+>
terminal.focus: 切换到命令行窗口 shift+alt/command+0
workbench.action.terminal.moveToEditor: 将命令窗口移动到编辑器 shift+alt/command+<
workbench.action.terminal.moveToTerminalPanel: 将命令窗口移动到侧边栏 shift+alt/command+>
breadcrumbs.focus: 聚焦到面包屑导航 shift+alt/command+;
